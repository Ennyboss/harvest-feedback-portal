Welcome!

This page introduces HARVEST, a digital agriculture tool developed by ICICLE to help agricultural stakeholders make smarter field decisions. Please watch the short videos, read the 5-Step Guide, and share your feedback in a brief survey. Your input will help make the tool more practical and user-friendly.

  • Complete a short role-based feedback form (takes 3–5 minutes).
